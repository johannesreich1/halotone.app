Questions

How do I restore Halotone Plus on another device?

Sign in with the Apple Account you bought it with. In Halotone, open the menu at the top, tap Musician mode, then tap Restore Purchase. Plus is shared with your family through Family Sharing.

What does Halotone Plus add?

Musician mode with keys, tempo in BPM, chord progressions, and melody editing; fifteen extra sounds; and up to 48 sounds in every song. Without Plus, each song holds twelve sounds, and Keys and Clap are free extra sounds. Plus is paid once.

Where are my songs saved?

On your device, inside Halotone, and from version 1.1 also in your private iCloud, so they appear on your other devices. Deleting the app deletes them from that device.

What do I need?

An iPhone or iPad with iOS 26 or later. Halotone follows your device's language in 22 languages.
